ArtyA7-100T_BSP - Digilent Arty A7-100T Board Support Pack
===========================================================

Declares the Digilent Arty A7-100T FPGA development board, mounted with a
synthesised SiFive E31 RISC-V core (RV32IMAC), requiring SiFive_E_DFP.

This pack contains no example/source content - it is a board pack only. See
the PicoJPEG example pack for actual usage.
